Project

Services
Editorial design · Typography · Art direction
Year

Overview

The idea behind the work.

This editorial project focuses on typography as the primary organizing device. Large headlines, generous margins and image-led spreads create a calm reading rhythm.

The layout system uses a clear hierarchy so cover lines, pull quotes, captions and long-form copy can coexist without competing for attention.

Visual direction

Building the language.

Editorial design is a conversation between words, images and the space around them.
